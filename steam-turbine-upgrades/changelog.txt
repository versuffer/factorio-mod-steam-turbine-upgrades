Version: 1.0.0
Date: 01. 11. 2026
  Features:
    - Added MK2 steam turbine: 60 MW by default, 5x7 tiles (200 kg per unit - a full stack fits into one rocket).
    - Added MK3 steam turbine: 140 MW by default, 7x10 tiles (1 ton per unit - one unit per rocket).
    - Both turbines consume steam in proportion to their power output (~10.3 steam/s per MW), like a row of vanilla turbines; steam hotter than 500°C gives no bonus.
    - Power output of both turbines is configurable in mod settings (MK2: 10-100 MW, MK3: 16-250 MW; steam consumption scales with it).
    - MK2 technology requires heating tower with Space Age, nuclear power otherwise. MK3 requires MK2.
    - Separated recipe ingredient sets for Space Age (with tungsten plates) and vanilla chains; the MK3 recipe costs exactly 3x the MK2 recipe.
